07 · native disclosure

Open the notebook.

The accordion uses native details/summary, so its copy remains present and readable even before scripting.

What makes a surface feel physical?⌄

Keep light direction consistent: upper-left highlights describe the material, while a lower-right contact edge anchors it. Wood, paper, moss, leather, and brass should each get a different kind of response.

What belongs in the shared API?⌄

Use the stable cc-* class names for structure and modifiers, then keep project-specific illustration classes beside them. The landing room’s scene remains its own preserved artwork.
