07 / disclosure

Details can stay
under a fold.

What does the inset surface mean?

Use it for a related group that is present but not asking to be noticed first. Its shadow falls inward, like a shallow ceramic well.

Why are the corners a little uneven?

The asymmetry is a material cue, not a usability tax. Borders, labels, and target sizes stay predictable even when the paper edge does not.

What happens without JavaScript?

The room link, navigation, native form controls, notices, and every tab panel remain in the document. Tabs become a visible set of panels; the accordion remains native HTML.
