# Cozy Bikes API

Live Citi Bike (New York and Jersey City) stations, the chance a bike or dock is
still there when you arrive, and trip plans. Public data, no auth, GET only.
Base URL: https://api.cozycommons.dev/bikes   Schemas: https://api.cozycommons.dev/openapi.json

Every endpoint reads Citi Bike's live feed, at most a minute or two old
(`age_s` says how old). Times without an offset are New York time. Coordinates
must be in the Citi Bike area (lat 40.45 to 40.95, lng -74.30 to -73.70). Add
`format=text` to /wait and /plan for one sentence to relay to the user.

## Find a station by name

GET /stations?q=broadway e 14&limit=5
GET /stations?q=west 31st street 7th avenue
GET /stations?q=broadway&lat=40.7497&lng=-73.9915     (nearest first)
-> stations: [{station, station_id, lat, lng, bikes, ebikes, classic_bikes, open_docks, docks_disabled,
   renting, returning, reported_s (age of the station's own report), stale}]

Stations are named by cross streets ("W 31 St & 7 Ave"), not landmarks. Street
words are normalized (west -> W, 31st -> 31, avenue -> Ave). For a landmark or
address ("Union Square", "Penn Station"), use its coordinates with lat and lng.

Use the `station_id` anywhere a station is accepted below.

## One station now and soon

GET /stations/{station_id}
-> live counts, chance.{bike,ebike,dock}.{15_min,30_min,60_min} (null when out
   of service), wait_if_empty_min (until someone returns a bike), wait_if_full_min
   (until someone takes one)

## Nearest stations: where do I get a bike (or dock)?

GET /wait?lat=40.7497&lng=-73.9915&need=bike        need: bike | ebike | dock
GET /wait?station=6331.01&need=dock&limit=5         start from a station instead
GET /wait?lat=...&lng=...&at=2026-07-15T17:00       any time: history only, no live counts
-> best (lowest expected minutes), stations: [{walk_min, bikes, ebikes, open_docks,
   status (available|empty|full|closed|unknown), chance (has it when you arrive),
   wait_min (refill wait if it does not), expected_min}], message

## Plan a trip

GET /plan?from_lat=40.7497&from_lng=-73.9915&to_station=5492.05
GET /plan?from_station=6331.01&to_lat=40.7295&to_lng=-73.9965&depart=2026-11-06T08:30&ebike=true
-> best: {start, end (each with walk_min, live counts, chance), ride_min, expected_min},
   alternatives, walk_only_min, message. Live odds cover departures up to an hour ahead.

## Every station at once

GET /live
-> columnar arrays: id, name, lat, lng, bikes, ebikes, docks, docks_disabled, reported_s,
   flags (1 renting, 2 returning, 4 stale)

## Notes

- Odds come from a year of trips (arrivals and departures by station, hour, day
  type and season) applied to the live count. Rebalancing vans are not in the data.
- Station reports are usually 1-4 minutes old. A station silent for 15 minutes is
  `stale` (status `unknown`): its counts may be hours old, so it gets no odds and
  is never recommended or planned through.
- `open_docks` already excludes broken docks; `docks_disabled` counts them.
- 404 means no such station in service; 503 means Citi Bike's feed is down.
